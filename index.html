About Skill Swap

Our Vision

Skill Swap is built on a simple idea: everyone has something to learn, and everyone has something to teach. We aim to create a global economy of knowledge exchange.

Community Driven

Whether you are a professional developer, a language enthusiast, or a beginner looking for guidance, our platform connects you with the right mentor without any money involved.

Time-Banking System

We use a unique Credit System. Earn credits by teaching your skills to others, and spend those credits to learn new skills from experts. It's a fair, time-for-time trade.

How it Works

1. Create Profile

Sign up, choose your role, and list the skills you want to teach or learn.

2. Find a Match

Discover users around the platform who match your learning interests.

3. Start Swapping

Connect via live chat, exchange knowledge, and grow together.
